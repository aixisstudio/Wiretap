Wiretap vs Little Snitch vs GlassWire vs Portmaster vs LuLu¶

English | Français

Where each one wins¶

Little Snitch — the most polished per-app firewall on macOS, bar none. Deep rules UI, research assistant, years of refinement. If you want the smoothest macOS-only experience and don't need source access, packet inspection or an API, it's the safe pick. Wiretap exists because there was no Little Snitch for Windows/Linux that is also open-source and inspects traffic, not just flows.

GlassWire — the prettiest bandwidth/usage graphs on Windows and an easy mobile companion. Good "what did my PC do yesterday" visualization. It is closed-source with paid tiers; no packet inspection and less of a privacy stance.

Portmaster (Safing) — the closest philosophical sibling: FOSS, local-first, great DNS-level filtering with filter lists, and a paid SPN network. Stronger than Wiretap on DNS ad/tracker blocking out of the box; weaker on raw packet visibility — Wiretap shows you the actual bytes (TLS ClientHello, DNS questions, JA3 fingerprints) and lets you write rules on them.

LuLu — the minimalist, free macOS outbound firewall from Objective-See. Excellent if all you want is "block app X from phoning home" on a Mac with zero cruft. No inspection, no cross-platform, no API — by design.

Wiretap — the only one of the five that combines: cross-platform FOSS, passive TLS fingerprinting (JA3/JA4) for C2 beacon detection, pluggable threat-intel feeds (ThreatFox/abuse.ch, your own lists), a scriptable authenticated API, SIEM exports, and per-process context — all local, no account, no vendor telemetry. The trade-off: the UI is younger than Little Wiretap's, and kernel-level enforcement varies by OS (pf/netsh/nftables under the hood — see FIREWALL.md for the honest matrix).

One-line summary¶

  • Need the most polished macOS-only firewall → Little Snitch
  • Need bandwidth graphs on Windows → GlassWire
  • Need DNS ad/tracker blocking + optional VPN-like SPN → Portmaster
  • Need a minimal free macOS outbound blocker → LuLu
  • Need open, cross-platform, inspectable traffic + C2 detection → Wiretap
